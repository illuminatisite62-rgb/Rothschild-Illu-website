Frequently Asked

Questions & Answers

How will I receive my benefits?

After your fee is processed, you will be contacted with full details regarding your transfer. Funds may be sent as a single payment or in instalments via bank transfer, or as a physical delivery of cash.

Why cannot the fee be deducted from my benefits?

The initiation fee confirms your commitment and allows our administrative processes to begin. This is a foundational requirement for all new members, without exception, and cannot be offset against your starter package.

Is this process confidential?

All membership proceedings are conducted with the utmost discretion. Your personal information is held in absolute confidence and will never be disclosed to any third party outside of the organisation.

How long does the process take?

The Phase I initiation typically takes place within days of your application being received and reviewed. Your Phase II ceremony is then scheduled within 15 days of your financial package being disbursed.
